# [Case Name] — Business-State Diagnosis and AI Opportunity Assessment Report

Business scope: [content]; investigation and data cutoff: [content]; version: [content]; status: [under investigation / preliminary recommendation / verified — list the real limitations].

## 1. The requester's original problem

Keep the original wording, the requesting role, the triggering situation, what they want changed, and the constraints; present feelings, known facts, and the requester's technical idea separately. State in everyday business language what this round needs to find out.

## 2. Business background and investigation scope

First explain how this business runs, whom it serves, and what business result it produces, then use a concrete matter to help an unfamiliar reader. State the roles, period, sampling method, and what is and is not covered by the investigation; identify the sources of the records.

## 3. Current-state business flow and organization

Show the unified current-state flow and the responsibilities: who uses which material or system, what they judge, and how it continues or returns. Present the normal path together with representative branches; keep what the policy requires, what the system implements, what people actually do, and the differences among them separate. Every important judgment about the flow must be traceable back to a specific record or asset.

## 4. Data, systems, and enterprise conditions

Show the system relationships, business objects, primary-key joins, fields, and statuses, and state the data quality, update timing, access conditions, and how usable the interfaces genuinely are. Expand the acquired assets, their purpose, and representative relations under the actual classification, linking to the complete asset inventory and the field dictionary. Explain separately what has not yet been obtained and what is restricted; "see the folder for the materials" is not acceptable.

## 5. Business baseline and problem diagnosis

First summarize the problems plainly, then explain item by item the current handling method, the extra labor or waiting, the evidence-supported cause, the impact, and the boundary of normal control. For each metric, note the period, unit, sample, denominator, source, formula, and computation run version. Show the actual charts and the per-metric conclusions, distinguishing spoken estimates, observation, and system measurement; mark unknown values explicitly as unknown or not measured; never replace them with zero.

## 6. AI opportunities and value assessment

Starting from the business cause, compare ordinary process/rule/system improvements against what AI adds. Form a suitable combination around information foundation building, decision support, and automation with clear boundaries; do not force an ontology. Write each candidate as "what it reads, what it does, what result it gives to whom," stating the basis for the value, the conditions required, and the limitations; without measured benefit, do not promise an ROI.

## 7. Business steps recommended for improvement and verification focus

State directly this round's recommendation, the reason for the choice, the deferred directions, the business scope, and the key unknowns still affecting the conclusion. State which business results and workload measures will judge whether it is worth continuing, and the comparison definitions relative to the existing baseline. Keep to the depth of an opportunity recommendation; do not invent an approved detailed development plan.

## Appendix: complete business assets

Under this case's actual classification, expand each asset's ID, content, source, effective version, and a working file link; the field dictionary, input data, computation scripts, results, and observation records must all be locatable. The complete inventory may be referenced while still showing a count and acquisition-status overview here. Keep the report output version consistent with the asset and computation versions, mark the relations still needing updates, and never treat an old fact from a historical report as current by default.
