## 2. Business rules and handling procedures

Register rules, policies, procedures, and their effective periods as asset entries in the same way; keep formal rules, local practice, and spoken understanding separate, and never judge validity automatically by a file's modification time.

## 3. Business records and originals

Group by the specific business record and register originals, supplemented versions, real screenshots, and communication records; keep the materials before and after handling comparable, and never overwrite an original.

## 4. System data and interface documentation

Register system exports, field dictionaries, status events, interface documentation, and access conditions. Having read the documentation does not mean the interface is connected or that writes are permitted; state the real verification status separately.

## 5. Products of the investigation

Register interview write-ups, problem records, the current-state flow, observation sheets, and computed derivatives, stating which originals they came from; generated material must never be treated in reverse as independent original evidence.
