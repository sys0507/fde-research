# Deliverables, numeric consistency, and human review

## Required outputs

Deliver the customer implementation-plan/quotation in Markdown and editable DOCX. Open with recommended scope, team, duration/milestones, service price, tax, client coding API/other expenses, separately procured equipment, and conditions. Explain decisions in prose, not only tables. Status stays pending human review/submitted, never a fictitious contract.

Use actual project terminology. Keep course IDs, teaching development, and test-production history outside the customer body while truthfully identifying proposed, unapproved, estimated, and unmeasured conditions.

The external `deliverables-and-acceptance.md` contains deliverable ID, requirement/package, included/excluded work, inputs/environment, action, observable pass rule, evidence, executor/confirmer, and failure treatment. Make monetary and authority boundaries explicit without requiring acceptance per code function.

Internal calculations preserve effort basis, roles/people, cost rates, payers, risk, margins, dates, and expense periods. Independent estimates use the structured model/results; inherited mode uses baseline.json and snapshots plus available original workbooks. Missing details remain missing, never invented. Prose cannot maintain competing numbers.

Separate `assumptions-and-basis.md` externally and internal assumptions/cost rationale. Customer-impacting conditions also appear in the report; appendices must not hide limits. Mark internal records “Vendor internal — exclude from customer distribution.”

Input index records versions/locations. Delivery index records relative paths, use, access, SHA256, generated/checked status. Rendering records bind final DOCX, page count, inspection, issues, and findings. Originals, models, cost CSVs, page images, and logs are not automatically client artifacts.

## Main report contents

1. Review summary: a primary recommendation and decisions, with input basis.
2. Scope/results: business capability, shared construction, integrations, inclusions/exclusions, acceptance linkage.
3. Organization/method: roles, windows, ownership, work/dependencies, milestones, constraints, parallelism. Hours may be public; internal rates are not.
4. Cooperation: customer data/interfaces/resources/authority/people, latest needed milestones, delay effects.
5. Acceptance: staged gates, early risks, separate development/acceptance sets, real environment/pilot, failures and confirming roles.
6. Price/payment: lines/totals/tax/validity/proposed milestones, client extras, recurring/unknown/excluded expenses.
7. Handoff/support: source/configuration, data/knowledge, tests/operations, support term/boundary.
8. Assumptions/change: grounded contingency, sensitivity, changes, and actual review status.

## Numerical generation

New estimates pass calculation before report generation. Inheritance preserves frozen values/original granularity and checks available relations without forcing a new model.

Use public render tokens for customer numbers, internal figures only internally. Do not recalculate money, labor, working days, payments, or line totals in prose. Additional public quantities need a generated/sourced table; business sample counts come from evidence, not arbitrary model quantities.

Expand all writing prompts. Check results/model version bindings and hashes, then reconcile key price/date/hour/line/payment/payer facts. Scripts cannot prove the meaning of every handwritten number. MD changes require rebuilding DOCX.

Do not disclose internal rates, margin/markup formulas, or internal links. Service lines are selling prices. Client API usage, price date/source, and budget are valid customer details because the customer pays them.

## Completion checks

Mechanical checks cover valid model, conservation/totals, daily capacity, dependency order, IDs/shared work, included scope coverage, payments, no overwrite, readable paths, and actual MD/DOCX. Passing tool tests is not validation of a project's model.

Judgment checks cover complete shared/incremental work, no double-charged normal repair, grounded risks, executable acceptance, disclosed customer conditions, protected internal numbers, and a reviewable recommendation.

Word checks require current all-page rendering, legible text/tables, repeating headers, heading associations, page/TOC accuracy, and final hash. Missing renderer means generated but layout-unverified; missing generation dependencies means DOCX missing. Deliver other work and clear recovery steps without claiming a complete formal Word.

## Required depth

Explain what, how, who/when, acceptance evidence, included fees, and treatment of changed conditions. After the summary provide substantive reasons and concrete arrangements, not another short summary/table collection. Appendices carry bulk detail; the body explains decisions.

No page target. Avoid both “confirm later” replacing a recommendation and vendor assumptions pretending to be customer approval. Customer documents exclude payroll/employer costs/margins/internal pricing disputes; internal documents reconcile reservation and active effort fully.

Check actual FDE/agent collaboration, payroll-period coverage, no client API margin duplication, no invented OCR fees, no unknown resources as zero, and TOC matched to final pagination. Mechanical success alone is not commercial maturity.
