# Estimating assumptions and basis

Fill project, version, date, applicable files, and actual status. Produce customer/internal variants; customer output excludes internal rates, costs, and margins. Where a usable estimate is justified, give the proposed value and impact rather than only “pending.”

## A-ID: subject

- Status: fact / skill assumption / human-confirmed / revised; source and time.
- Visibility: customer / vendor internal.
- Adopted condition: explicit basis usable for calculation or scheduling.
- Reason/basis: why needed and which quantities, records, or judgment support it.
- Applicability/impact: requirements, packages, capacity, cost/dates, acceptance.
- Verification: which role returns which evidence by which milestone.
- Change treatment: trigger and affected work/cost/plan; whether commercial confirmation is required.

End with a few priority review matters, not an empty questionnaire.
