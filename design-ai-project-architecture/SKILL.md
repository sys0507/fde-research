## Workflow

### 1. Identify technical responsibilities

Read [interaction and conditions](references/interaction-and-conditions.md). Inherit readiness conclusions; extract tasks, targets, load, data location, authoritative systems, responsibilities, and resources. Merge shared capabilities while preserving requirement mappings. Decompose tasks to understand inputs, judgments, actions, results, and side effects, not to manufacture a file per scene.

Maintain the internal conditions/open-issues document. Distinguish confirmed conditions, design assumptions, dependencies awaiting provision, and critical unknowns. Engineering assumptions can support design; permissions and formal business approval cannot be assumed. Do not re-ask known facts or equate “can apply” with “allocated.”

### 2. Research and recommend

Read [selection methods](references/selection-method.md). Apply six areas as relevant: models/deployment, documents/knowledge, agent frameworks, task organization, capability extension, and application/operations. They are reasoning dimensions, not a mandatory shopping list.

Compare candidates solving the same problem against effects/constraints, explaining mechanism, effectiveness, cost, and current choice. Meet necessary effects, performance, and hard constraints before comparing investment. Explain routine choices briefly and core AI tradeoffs deeply. Do not sacrifice required capability for simplicity or recommend complexity for novelty.

Verify changing versions, official capabilities, deployment, licenses, limits, and material prices. Record date, source, and support scope. Distinguish vendor claims, public evaluations, project measurements, and inference. Evaluate geographical/service availability from actual conditions, not a fixed vendor list, parameter count, or popularity.

For consequential choices give the recommendation, component responsibility, evidence, investment, remaining verification, and switch trigger. Add alternatives only for genuine differences, without forcing three options or “anything works.” Start the report without component-by-component FDE approval; distinguish recommendation, FDE choice, and enterprise approval.

“Reuse an internal model service” determines a route, not a model choice. Recommend a specific version or identifiable family/configuration; list access, quota, and actual internal supply separately. Search current families and recent releases, not only remembered pages. If evidence cannot support an identifiable recommendation, explain the limitation and unresolved decision; “choose later” is not completed selection.

### 3. Bounded questions

Allow at most 20 proactive question rounds awaiting answers, normally one main decision and at most one or two directly related clarifications. State known facts/impact first; decision questions include a recommendation/reason. Research professional questions yourself; ask the FDE about enterprise facts and consequential choices.

Track rounds across resumptions. Do not pack independent questions together or restart a run to evade the cap. If the user cannot answer, define an evidence task and continue supported work. At the cap deliver the complete conditional design and open issues, without a 21st proactive round or fabricated confirmation.

### 4. Integrate architecture and development

Read [system design](references/system-design.md). Walk representative paths through entry, identity/scope, processing, tools/data, results, persistence, failures, and human handoff. Check compatibility, available interfaces, storage responsibilities, resources, and permission/model/data boundaries.

Provide depth for review and work breakdown without implementing the system. Specify packages, dependencies, deliverables, critical validation, and resources, separating planned/measured capability. Detailed individual calendars, quotations, and margins belong to planning. Separate development API use from production operation; internal services are not automatically free.

### 5. Produce diagrams and the formal report

Read [architecture delivery](references/architecture-delivery.md) and fulfill the explicit `diagram_profile`. Standard delivery includes editable sources, actually rendered static images, and verification records. Archify additionally requires interactive HTML and a real receipt. Split complexity into readable views. Verify diagrams/browser behavior; recommendations are not current production.

Write complete source using the [report outline](assets/report-outline.md), then generate and genuinely render DOCX according to [Word delivery](references/word-delivery.md). Prefer the customer template; otherwise use a professional formal format. Choose A4/A3 diagram pages by density, without fixed counts. Actual interactive-page screenshots may be embedded. Avoid encyclopedia prose, internal debugging, and invented signatures.

Use the [conditions template](assets/conditions-template.md) internally. Conditions affecting customer investment, cooperation, scope, or outcomes also belong in the formal report. Never hide material conditions internally or publish internal questions, raw data, or credentials in diagram-serving folders.

### 6. Verify and deliver

Generate `delivery-manifest.json` using the run contract and perform mechanical checks; these do not replace semantic review. Check requirement ownership, consequential-choice evidence, agreement among report/diagrams/conditions/development/validation, and unexpanded placeholders. Inspect every final DOCX page and meet the selected diagram contract. Stop after targeted repairs and checks of main paths/known defects.

Deliver formal Word, internal conditions MD, diagram sources/static images, optional Archify interaction, and concise evidence/resumption records. Call it complete only when all required artifacts are usable; otherwise state partial delivery and recovery actions. Record actual checks, not business success inferred from exit status. Report serving/LAN availability only if verified.

## Changes and boundaries

Without authorization do not connect production, send messages, purchase, run paid tests, or develop/deploy business systems. Outputs are not enterprise approval.

Adapt methods without deleting requirements, hiding unknowns, skipping formal Word, or passing standard diagrams off as Archify. Retain recoverable work under runtime limits; never invent research, captures, measurements, or answers.
