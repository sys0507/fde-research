# Inherited assets, bounded questions, and design conditions

Keep architecture grounded in actual materials and explicit assumptions while limiting dialogue burden. Apply this throughout selection, design, reporting, and revision.

The questioning approach draws on decision dependencies in [Grill Me / grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md) and recommendation-led discussion in [GStack plan-eng-review](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/tree/main/plan-eng-review). This file provides a complete adapted FDE method; installing/executing those projects is unnecessary. Their unlimited questions, individual approvals, telemetry, or automatic actions are not inherited.

## 1. Effective input baseline

Record user scope, output location, and existing decisions. Originals are read-only; do not reorganize or overwrite another agent's assets. Distinguish formal requirements/changes, facts/originals, conditions/answers, technical material, public research, project experiments, and simulated inputs.

For consequential inputs retain path, version/date, effective status, use, and optionally a hash. “Final” in a filename proves no authority. Resolve versions in order: user-designated baseline/choices/change scope; formal requirements and explicit amendments; readiness evidence supplementing implementation without silently reducing requirements; then targeted resolution of equally authoritative conflicts that affect design.

Inherit established authority, load, and resources. Reconfirm only for changed scope, conflicts, expired conditions, or new calls beyond prior authority. Complete reading means understanding all effective requirements/conditions, using structured parsing and material samples rather than stuffing every attachment into context. Unreadable files require recovery attempts and an impact record; existence is not verified content.

## 2. Facts, choices, and authority

| Information | Supports | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| A resource may be requested | Application route | Allocation, sufficient quantity, ongoing availability |
| Interface documentation | Specified capability | Production access/current identity authorization |
| FDE accepts a recommendation | Current project technical choice | Customer, procurement, or security approval |
| A few samples pass | Those samples/conditions | Population accuracy/production acceptance |
| Promise of sanitized data | Planned provision | Receipt, effective sanitization, unrestricted transfer |
| Oral latency estimate | Scoped estimate | Measurement/contract SLA |

Oral statements, owner decisions, and estimates can support work with role/date/scope/content/basis/status. Use an actual role if the name is unknown. Simulated conditions may support a complete teaching design but not a claim of real investigation.

## 3. When to ask the FDE

First use inputs, previous answers, and research. Ask when missing internal facts change architecture; material requirements conflict; viable routes involve consequential effect/cost/responsibility/scope choices; or an unmet condition requires choosing which constraint can change.

Do not transfer professional judgment by asking “which model/framework do you want?” Recommend first, explain the actual choice. Order questions by decision dependency: processing boundary before hosted service, action authority before tool details. Use explicit assumptions for ordinary independent parameters. Continue independent research/mapping/writing while awaiting answers, without performing dependent unauthorized actions.

## 4. At most 20 proactive rounds

Each proactive question followed by waiting counts, including clarification and choices. A main decision with one or two directly related clarifications may share a round; independent questions cannot be packed together to evade the cap.

Reading, search, analysis, progress updates, and unsolicited user information do not count. A resulting proactive follow-up does. Unanswered questions still count; silence is no answer or approval.

Natural format: explain existing evidence, specific gap, affected choice, recommendation/reason, then request one main fact/choice. Decision options should be real and mutually exclusive; factual questions must not preselect approved/enabled answers.

Example: “The current material requires manager review but does not establish write-back authority. I recommend producing verifiable results and leaving formal submission in the source system for the first phase. Does the confirmed scope allow read-only access or also human-confirmed writes?”

When the user does not know, record responsible role, evidence, needed milestone, and a conditional route; do not keep rephrasing. Preserve partial answers and ask again only for remaining material differences, counting that follow-up.

## 5. At the cap

Prioritize overall-design conditions as the cap approaches and use explicit assumptions for ordinary settings. After round 20 deliver supported formal report, diagrams, and internal conditions without another proactive question. Conditions remain conditional, not confirmed because questioning ended.

If critical data permission, interfaces, or essential operating conditions lack evidence and alternatives, identify what cannot be finalized and deliver a conditional design alongside complete supported parts. Assumptions never authorize external data transfer, production writes, purchases, or paid experiments.

## 6. Four condition categories

Always deliver `architecture-conditions-and-open-issues.md` for the FDE/technical/delivery team:

- **Confirmed foundations:** actual source/scope/version. An identity protocol may be available while the project account is still pending.
- **Engineering assumptions:** value/range, reason, scope, sensitivity, calibration. A planning concurrency supports queue/capacity design, not a customer guarantee.
- **Dependencies awaiting provision:** direction known but quota/account/document/equipment/cooperation outstanding. State role, deliverable, milestone, and fallback. Internal-model application availability does not mean a project quota exists.
- **Critical unknowns:** could change architecture, outcome commitment, or scope. Give conditional recommendation, evidence task, and alternatives. “Data stays in-domain” does not prove a group service lies within that domain.

Choose one primary state per matter with brief change history; do not copy it into four categories. With no unknowns, retain a concise baseline without invented tasks.

## 7. Actionable conditions

For each material condition state what is known/basis; affected decisions/components/resources/checks/commitments; who can confirm (role or “to be assigned”); evidence/result required; actual milestone; and what changes if false, including work/scope implications. Use short sections/narrow tables, not ten-column grids. Cite the file or specific answer.

## 8. Internal/external disclosure

Internal separation supports collaboration, not hidden premises. Conditions affecting customer investment, cooperation, scope, outcomes, or timing also enter the formal report. Keep sensitive addresses, detailed follow-up, and internal discussions private; public diagram folders must not contain them or originals.

Explain conditions intelligibly, e.g. capacity depends on an agreed load test with a stated expansion route. “Discussed” or “agreed in principle” cannot close an unclear scope question.

## 9. Continuation and change propagation

Read input baseline, ledger, conditions, decisions, and delivery status. Preserve choices/authority and question counts across chats/runs. Trace changed facts through requirement/condition → technical decision → component/path → work → validation/resources → report/diagrams. Keep unaffected choices.

If an external service becomes prohibited, revisit model, knowledge compilation, external observability, and development-data routes—not just diagram labels. Mark replaced assumptions and effects; an accepted tradeoff is a decision, not an experiment.

## 10. Final check

Check inheritance rather than repeated investigation, round limit, actionable unknowns, honest assumptions, external disclosure, and propagated changes. Fix findings without another blanket approval round.
