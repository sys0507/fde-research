# Asset organization and final handoff

The stage delivers interaction records, locatable business/technical assets, and a final user-facing handoff. Maintain the first two on intake; automatically produce the third when design evidence is sufficient, without another request.

## Preserve what actually arrives

Reuse existing folders, originals, and manifests. Register local files at their actual paths; organize newly saved material by provider/use. Inputs determine categories/counts. Do not move/rename/copy everything merely to satisfy a template; repair references if a move is necessary.

- **Originals:** preserve files or accessible locations, provider, intake round, date, object/environment. Separate receipt date from business cutoff; unknown metadata remains unknown.
- **Role explanations:** save chat/oral answers with provider, subject, exact content, basis, scope, and status. Distinguish provider text from FDE paraphrase. Reuse existing records; ordinary planning is not automatically a signature process.
- **Derived materials:** create field/relationship/estimate tables only when useful, with sources, extraction scope, and transformations. Organization cannot invent facts, authority, prices, or conclusions or alter originals to match judgments.
- **Missing items:** register gaps/evidence tasks, never empty files pretending to be acquired. Record unavailable evidence and treatment.

Keep permission scope, applications, decisions, activation, and verification states, not passwords/tokens/private keys. Use approved storage without unauthorized transfer. Analyze real inputs; do not manufacture enterprise responses.

## Unified asset entry

Maintain a lightweight machine-readable index, e.g. `readiness-asset-index.json`, or extend an equivalent existing one. It locates facts/status rather than duplicating them.

| Content | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Original business assets | Current baseline locations/version/scope or existing inventory link |
| New material packages | Actual location/manifest; counts exclude indexes and helper descriptions |
| New entries | Stable ID, path, question/use, provider/date, nature/scope, directly or via source manifest |
| Current conclusions | Final handoff, latest issue state, coverage/evidence appendices; retain existing hashes |
| Selection rules | Active state, replacements/missing items, base for relative paths |

Later answers update only explicitly covered matters/objects/time/scope. Record replacements, preserve unresolved conflicts, and treat old missing-item lists as historical snapshots. Current status retains known facts, assumptions, limitations, deferred checks, Q IDs, and meanings. Link one authoritative state instead of copying a drifting condition table.

Update index/state as evidence arrives. After handoff, update the final report only for relevant changes and retain rounds.

## Final report

At closure use the [handoff template](../assets/handoff-report-template.md) to create `implementation-conditions-and-design-handoff.md` at the requested handoff location or readiness root. Cross-link it and the index. This is normal completion, not an extra approval step.

Open with whether candidate comparison/estimation can begin, whether further grouped questions are needed, and applicable scope. Use five parts: results/scope, established conditions, limitations/treatment, estimating assumptions/later checks, next design tasks/asset entry. Explain effects on design/work/cost. Each next task says which acquired inputs support which decision and output.

Make it independently readable, with few internal IDs and no repeated full questionnaire. Link sources/appendices when necessary. Identify original/new directories, material packages, and machine-readable index, not “see attachments” without paths or a machine index with no human guide. Do not preselect final technology, price, or development commitments.

Check report/state agreement, sourced key judgments, accessible paths, and usable assumptions/checks. Readiness does not mean access opened or effects accepted. The skill finishes only after final report and usable index are delivered; do not reopen investigation just to close every issue.
